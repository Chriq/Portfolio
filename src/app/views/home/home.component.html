What is Scaredy Cat?

Scaredy Cat is an expression of my aesthetic taste, striking a delicate balance between the fun & whimsical, and the creepy & unsettling. My love of games was born in the nightmare fuel of Legend of Zelda games (particularly Ocarina of Time and Majora's Mask). It was nurtured in the existential dread of OMORI and OneShot. It matured in the philosophical pondering of Wanderstop and Clair Obscur: Expedition 33.

Across the 20 games I've made (and counting!), I have honed my skills as a developer, designer, storyteller, and leader. My experience has helped me to explore themes of identity, agency, conviction, and personal reality in my games. Would you journey with me to discover these strange, wonderful, and sometimes horrifying parts of being human? Games have much to teach us about ourselves. All you have to do is play, listen, and don't be a scaredy cat!
